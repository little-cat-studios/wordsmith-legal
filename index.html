1. Who this notice covers

This notice describes how Wordsmith may process information when you use a development or test build of the game, including guest play, signed-in play, Daily Challenge and social features where enabled, and advertising consent tools from Google.

2. Information we may process

Depending on how you use the game, we may process:

  • Account and identity — internal player identifiers (including for guest play), email if you create or link an account, sign-in provider (email, Google, and/or Apple where available), display name, and sign-in or verification status.
  • Preferences — settings such as audio volumes, haptics, notification toggles, dark mode, and language.
  • Gameplay — Daily Challenge attempts, guesses, outcomes, streaks, statistics, and related timestamps (scheduling uses UTC). Practice mode is designed as a local client experience.
  • Social and safety — friendships, invites, Friend Lobby participation and scores, in-app notifications, achievements, blocks, and player reports you submit. Community features may remain unavailable depending on build or region.
  • Advertising and consent — when ads are enabled for your build, Google’s Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process device and advertising signals, consent or privacy-options choices, and ad load, show, click, or reward events. Rewarded-ad verification is designed to store verification references (such as provider transaction identifiers and integrity hashes) rather than raw secret payloads.
  • Messages you send us — content of emails or reports you submit to the contact address below.

We do not design Wordsmith to collect payment card numbers into our own databases. In-app purchases are not shipped in the audited development product path described here.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and secure the game, accounts, progress, and multiplayer or social features.
  • Show optional advertising and rewarded features (such as a seventh guess where enabled), including consent collection via UMP.
  • Verify eligible rewarded-ad completions before granting related in-game benefits where that path is configured.
  • Moderate reports and protect integrity of gameplay and rewards.
  • Respond to privacy or support requests sent to our contact email.

Client-side ad “reward” callbacks alone are not treated as authority to grant seventh-guess style rewards; server-side verification is part of the designed path where that infrastructure is configured.

4. Advertising and consent choices

Wordsmith uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform to present consent or privacy messaging where configured for the app’s AdMob App ID. The game is designed to fail closed: if required consent or ad eligibility is not available, advertising initialisation and optional ad offers are blocked while core gameplay remains usable.

Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, Wordsmith can expose a Privacy options control in Account Settings. You may also use operating-system advertising or tracking controls.

When ads are allowed but personalised advertising is not, the app is designed to request non-personalised ads (including an AdMob npa request extra). That does not by itself guarantee how Google serves creatives in every region, and it does not replace UMP or applicable law.

A UMP consent message is not a substitute for a complete privacy policy. iOS App Tracking Transparency is not described here as available; iOS advertising configuration remains incomplete for shipping.

5. Who may receive information

  • Supabase — authentication and game backend services used by Wordsmith.
  • Google — AdMob, UMP, and related advertising or sign-in services where used.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store distribution where used.
  • Studio operators — reviewing reports, securing the service, and responding to requests.

Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics packages were not present in the audited development package set; they should not be assumed to be shipping in current development builds unless a later build adds them.

Hosting regions and international-transfer safeguards for backend services are not listed on this interim page because they are not yet confirmed for publication.

6. Accounts, sign-out, retention, and deletion

  • Guest play still creates an anonymous account identifier so progress can be stored.
  • Signing out ends the current session and typically starts a new guest session; it does not by itself delete the previous account’s server-side data.
  • In-app self-service account deletion and data export are not available in the audited product. To request help with access, correction, or deletion, email us (see Contact).
  • Exact retention periods and the final deletion or anonymisation model are not yet published as commitments on this interim page.

7. Children’s privacy

Wordsmith is a word game with a broad audience. We have not published a final determination on this page that the service is or is not directed to children under 13 (or under other local digital-consent ages). We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information in a manner prohibited by applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information inappropriately.
